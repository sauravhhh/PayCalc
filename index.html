What each word means

Basic Pay

The fixed monthly salary of your post, decided by your pay level or pay scale. Everything else — DA, HRA, NPS — is calculated from this one number, so a higher basic lifts the whole salary.

DA — Dearness Allowance

Money to fight price rise (inflation). Central government and railway DA is 60% of basic right now and rises every January and July. Bank DA is 31.33% and changes every 3 months — lower in percentage only because a big part of it was merged into bank basic pay in the 12th Bipartite Settlement.

HRA — House Rent Allowance

Money for house rent, paid when the employer doesn't give you a house or quarters. Central government pays 30% / 20% / 10% of basic by city size (X / Y / Z). Banks pay a flat 10.25% everywhere; RBI pays 15%.

TA — Transport Allowance

A fixed monthly amount for your daily travel to duty. DA is also paid on top of it in most jobs.

Special Allowance — banks

An extra 26.5% of basic pay given under the bank wage settlement. One catch: it does not count towards your pension (NPS).

Running Allowance — railways

Paid only to running staff (loco pilots and guards) for every kilometre actually run — e.g. ₹430 per 100 km for an ALP, ₹537 per 100 km for a Goods Guard. Run more, earn more; that is why a guard's salary swings month to month.

NPS — pension deduction

10% of your (Basic + DA) is cut from your salary every month and put into your National Pension System account. Your employer adds another 14% from its side. You get it back as pension after retirement.

Gross vs In-hand

Gross is everything added together before any cut: Basic + DA + HRA + all allowances. In-hand is what reaches your bank account after NPS, income tax and professional tax are deducted. Websites usually quote gross; employees talk about in-hand.

About these numbers: Bank figures use the 12th Bipartite Settlement (DA 31.33% for Nov 2026 – Jan 2027); railway figures use 7th CPC with 60% DA and kilometrage rates revised March 2026; RBI Assistant basic ₹29,000 as per the revised 2026 scale. Clerk figures are for a graduate entrant (2 advance increments). Tax is an approximate monthly average under the new tax regime — your exact tax depends on your total yearly income.
