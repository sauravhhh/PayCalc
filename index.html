This section is only meanings — no numbers to change, nothing to calculate. Read it once, then go back to the Calculator tab.
Basic Pay

The fixed salary of your post. Everything else is calculated from this number.

DA — Dearness Allowance

Extra money because prices keep rising. When things get costlier, DA goes up.

Bank DA changes every 3 months. Government DA changes every 6 months.

HRA — House Rent Allowance

Money for house rent. Paid only if your office does not give you a house.

TA — Transport Allowance

Money for travelling to office every day.

Special Allowance — bank only

An extra percentage of Basic Pay, given in bank jobs. Not counted for pension.

Special Pay — bank clerk only

Extra money for working at the customer desk.

Grade Allowance + LCA — RBI only

RBI's own extra allowances. Other banks do not give these — one reason RBI pays more.

Running Allowance — railway only

Money for every kilometre the train runs. Only drivers and guards get it. Run more km, get more money.

MSP — defence only

Military Service Pay. Extra money only for Army, Navy and Air Force posts, for the hardship of service life. DA is paid on Basic + MSP.

Grade Pay — Assam only

Assam state jobs are shown as a pay band plus Grade Pay. Your Basic Pay is the two added together — Grade III: ₹14,000 + ₹6,200 = ₹20,200. Grade IV: ₹12,000 + ₹3,900 = ₹15,900. APSC (ACS): ₹30,000 + ₹13,300 = ₹43,300.

Medical Allowance — Assam only

A small fixed amount every month for medical expenses, paid to Assam state government staff.

Gross

Basic Pay + DA + HRA + all allowances added together. This is before any cut.

NPS — Pension cut

10% is cut from your salary and saved for your pension. Your office adds 14% more from its side.

Tax

Money paid to the government from your salary, plus a small professional tax.

In-hand

Gross minus NPS minus Tax. This is the money that actually comes to your bank account.

Basic Pay + DA + HRA + Allowances
= Gross
Gross − NPS − Tax = In-hand

Are the heads the same for every job? No. Basic Pay + DA + HRA + TA are common everywhere. The extra heads are different — bank has Special Allowance, RBI has Grade Allowance + LCA, railway has Running Allowance, Assam state jobs have Grade Pay and Medical Allowance.
